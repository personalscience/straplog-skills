# hrv-resting — metric interpretation

Read when you need norms or are explaining what a metric means.

| Metric | What it reflects | Rough adult norms |
|---|---|---|
| **HR** (mean) | Overall autonomic drive | 45–75 bpm resting |
| **SDNN** | Overall HRV (all sources, includes HR drift) | 20–100 ms for short reads |
| **RMSSD** | Short-term beat-to-beat variability · **vagal tone marker** | 15–80 ms |
| **LnRMSSD** | log(RMSSD) — more normally distributed; preferred for trend | 2.7–4.4 typical |
| **pNN50** | % of intervals differing >50 ms · another vagal proxy | 3–30 % |

Higher LnRMSSD usually = better recovery / parasympathetic activity. But the absolute number matters less than the user's own trend — day-to-day changes of 0.1–0.3 LnRMSSD units are normal noise; sustained drifts over 1–2 weeks are the real signal.
